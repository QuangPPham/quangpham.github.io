Studied the forward and inverse position analysis for spatial mechanisms. In the forward analysis, the position and orientation of the last link of the manipulator is determined based on knowledge of the manipulator joint parameters. In the reverse analysis, the joint parameters are determined that will position and orient the last link to a desired specified pose.
