I am an undergraduate Mechanical Engineering student at the University of Florida. I have pursued research at the [Active Perception and Robot Intelligence Lab](https://janeshin-website.github.io/) under the guidance of Dr. [Jane Shin](https://janeshin-website.github.io/people/jane/) where I studied **active perception** and **3D reconstruction**. I was also at the [Smart Autonomy Lab](https://faculty.eng.ufl.edu/smart-autonomy-lab/) advised by Dr. [Yu Wang](https://sites.google.com/view/y-wang), studying **LTL-based task planning** and **reinforcement learning**. Currently, I am very interested in **legged robot control**, particularly in combining model-based trajectory optimization and learning-based methods. Check out the other tabs to learn more about me, and feel free to reach out via email if you want to talk about anything!

Aside from reading papers or running simulations, I also do other fun stuff like going to the gym, playing soccer, playing mobile games, and watching Netflix (my favorite show is Dark, go check it out!).
